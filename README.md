# about.schiller.engineer...

I’m someone who enjoys looking behind the scenes, understanding how things fit together, and developing workable solutions to complex technical challenges.

My focus is on network architecture, IT infrastructure, IT security, and Linux-based systems. I’m particularly interested in robust, scalable, and—as far as possible—transparent architectures. From routing and switching to high availability and redundancy, all the way to security concepts and infrastructure modernization.

I thrive in technically challenging environments: whether dealing with complex network structures, live migrations, analyzing unusual error patterns, or designing new infrastructure concepts. I enjoy thinking beyond existing solutions, questioning established approaches, and testing things out in practice rather than relying solely on theoretical concepts.

A large part of my expertise is rooted in curiosity, self-directed learning, and the joy of truly understanding technical interrelationships. Open source, Linux, network protocols, security, and a focus on the big picture guide me in this work.

I’m not a fan of unnecessary complexity, overcomplicated processes, or solutions that look good on paper but don’t work in practice. What matters to me is technical substance, clean architecture, pragmatic implementation, and sustainable solutions.
